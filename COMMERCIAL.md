## Under the AGPL, free for everyone

You may use, study, change and share AuteurFrame for any purpose, including commercial work, as long as you follow the
AGPL. In practice:

- **Using it as it is.** Run it on your own machine, make films for yourself, your company or your clients, and publish
  them anywhere, including monetised channels. Nothing to publish, nothing to pay.
- **Your films are yours.** Rendered videos, posters and subtitles are your output and are not covered by the AGPL.
  Exported HTML bundles contain the AuteurFrame runtime, which stays under the AGPL when you share them.
- **Sharing a copy.** If you give anyone AuteurFrame or a modified version, include the full source code of your
  version under the AGPL, the [LICENSE](LICENSE), [LICENSE-EXCEPTIONS.md](LICENSE-EXCEPTIONS.md) and
  [NOTICE.txt](NOTICE.txt).
- **Running it as a service.** If you let people use a modified AuteurFrame over a network, such as a hosted studio,
  SaaS or API, you must offer those users the complete source code of your modified version under the AGPL.
- **Keeping the credit.** Modified versions keep the "AuteurFrame by Abdur Rahman Maheer" notice in their interface.

## When you need a commercial license

Buy a commercial license if you want to do any of these without the AGPL's conditions:

- offer AuteurFrame, or a product built on it, as a hosted service, SaaS or API **without** publishing your source code;
- embed AuteurFrame, its runtime, templates or skills in a closed-source or proprietary product;
- distribute a modified version without releasing your changes;
- remove the in-app "AuteurFrame by Abdur Rahman Maheer" attribution;
- get warranties, indemnity, support or terms your company's legal team requires.

If you are unsure which applies to you, ask. It costs nothing to ask.

## How to ask

1. Open an issue at <https://github.com/joyghoshs/auteurframe/issues> titled **Commercial license request**.
2. Say who you are, what you want to build, how it will be offered, and the expected users or revenue.
3. The author replies and agrees the terms with you in writing.

Only a written agreement signed by the author grants a commercial license. A reply on an issue, a star, a fork or a
merged pull request does not.

## Reporting misuse

If you find AuteurFrame hosted or sold without its source code, or without its attribution, open an issue with a
link or contact [@joyghoshs](https://github.com/joyghoshs). Under section 8 of the AGPL, a violator's rights end; someone
notified of a first violation keeps them only by fixing it within 30 days of the notice.
