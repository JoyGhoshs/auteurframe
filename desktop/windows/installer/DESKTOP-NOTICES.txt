  https://www.python.org/

  Build: https://www.gyan.dev/ffmpeg/builds/   Source: https://ffmpeg.org/download.html

Open media and fonts used by AuteurFrame are listed in THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md.

Where your work is stored
-------------------------
Projects, settings, API keys and logs live in %LOCALAPPDATA%\AuteurFrame. Uninstalling AuteurFrame
asks before deleting them.
